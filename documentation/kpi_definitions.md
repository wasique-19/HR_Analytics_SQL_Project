Define KPIs

A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is a measurable value tied to a business goal. A good KPI has: a clear name, a formula, a time period, and a business meaning.

#	KPI	Formula	Business meaning
1	Headcount	Count of active employees	Workforce size
2	Attrition Rate	Exits in period ÷ average headcount × 100	How fast people leave
3	Retention Rate	100 − Attrition Rate	How many stay
4	Average Tenure	Average years of service	Workforce stability
5	Avg Performance Rating	Mean of review scores	Overall performance level
6	High-Performer %	Employees rated ≥ 4 ÷ reviewed employees	Talent quality
7	Absenteeism Rate	Absent days ÷ total working days × 100	Productivity loss
8	Average Salary	Mean current salary	Compensation level
9	Salary Cost	Sum of current salaries	Budget impact
10	Compa-Ratio	Employee salary ÷ role midpoint salary	Pay fairness vs. benchmark
11	Promotion Rate	Promotions in year ÷ headcount × 100	Career growth
12	Avg Time to Promotion	Mean months between promotions	Growth speed
13	Early Attrition Rate	Exits within 12 months of hire ÷ total hires	Hiring/onboarding quality
14	Gender Pay Gap	(Avg male − avg female) ÷ avg male within same level	Equity
15	Regretted Attrition %	Exits of high performers ÷ total exits	Cost of losing top talent
