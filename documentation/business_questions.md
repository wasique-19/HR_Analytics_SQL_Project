Write the Business Questions

Business questions come before SQL. Aim for 25+. Starter list (you should add at least 5 of your own):

Workforce

How many employees are active vs. exited?
What is the headcount by department, location, and gender?
What is the average tenure by department?

Performance
4. What is the average performance rating by department?
5. Who are the top 10% performers?
6. Which managers have the highest-performing teams?
7. Does performance improve with tenure?

Attendance
8. What is the absenteeism rate by department?
9. Which months have the highest absences?
10. Is there a relationship between low attendance and low performance?

Compensation
11. What is the average salary by job role and department?
12. Are there large salary gaps within the same role?
13. Is the gender pay gap significant within the same job level?
14. How does salary relate to performance rating?

Promotions
15. How many employees were promoted each year?
16. What is the average time-to-promotion?
17. Are high performers actually being promoted?

Attrition
18. What is the overall attrition rate?
19. Which departments have the highest attrition?
20. What is the typical tenure at exit?
21. Do low-paid employees leave more?
22. Do low performers or high performers leave more?
23. What are the most common exit reasons?

Strategic
24. Which departments are "high risk" (high attrition + low performance)?
25. What is the estimated salary cost lost to attrition?
